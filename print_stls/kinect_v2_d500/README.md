# Kinect v2 swivel and LDROBOT D500 mount

Print one of each of the [nine main parts](parts). Part 01 combines the 189 × 120 mm base with the fixed D500 mast. The base plate is 6 mm thick and the LiDAR seat is 200 mm above its underside. The entire part is oriented for the Prusa i3 MK3 and slices at 189 × 120 × 200 mm. Use the supplied orientation, 100% scale and millimetres.

Start with the [fit and spacer files](fit_tests). Print the horn adapter and bearing gauges to check fit. The servo tray uses four 3 mm spacers in the nominal assembly. The 0.5, 1 and 2 mm spacers let you adjust the servo height to match your horn. The swivel uses two 6809 bearings (45 × 58 × 7 mm). The separate bearing spacer is part 04.

The prototype profile used PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, six walls and 45% gyroid infill. PrusaSlicer generated the compact base toolpath with supports disabled. Physical fit and strength still need checking. The D500 deck follows the STL-19P three-hole layout, 46.80 mm across the pair and 31.92 mm from the pair to the third hole. [LDROBOT mechanical drawing, page 7](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/D500-LiDAR-Kit/LDROBOT_STL-19P_Datasheet_EN_v1.0%281%29.pdf)
